## How I Work

1. Define the question and the decision the analysis should support.
2. Record data sources, collection dates, and usage constraints.
3. Compare against a baseline and examine the validation design.
4. Document results, limitations, and the steps needed to review them.

I am interested in applying these habits to company research, comparative analysis, and investment-support workflows. These are academic and personal projects, not a claim of professional investment execution or fund administration experience.
